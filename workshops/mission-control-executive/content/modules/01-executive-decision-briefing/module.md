# Mission Control: Executive AI Value Decision Briefing

This is the candidate text-only contract for initiative
`mission-control-executive-decision-briefing` at repository baseline
`3c588d7f08a5ab7952e8e64792e1faffad56bf30`.

## Decision outcome

The executive group tests whether the evidence supports the next responsible
decision for one bounded AI-development change. The running example is a
proposed pilot in one team's pull-request-based production change-control
workflow. The output is a stop, revise, fund, scale, or explicit defer record
with owners, resources, timeframe, dissent, dependencies, and next gate.

## Exact contract

- **Core slides:** 12
- **Instruction:** 30 minutes
- **Protected executive discussion:** 15 minutes
- **Total:** 45 minutes
- **Mission, media, separate setup/transition, breaks, contingency:** 0 minutes
- **Exact source and visible copy:**
  `content/modules/01-executive-decision-briefing/executive-decision-source.md`
- **Exact title/order/timing manifest:**
  `content/modules/01-executive-decision-briefing/slide-manifest.md`

The decision comes first. **Govern -> Improve -> Prove -> Decide** is supporting
wayfinding. It does not replace the fixed title order.
