## Claim and publication limits

- This contract introduces **no changed external product claim**, so no new
  external research or `research-provenance` record is required.
- The AI assistant, permissions, controls, and workflow are a customer-neutral
  planning example. The contract does not claim that a current product offers
  a named control, model, entitlement, price, quota, or enforcement behavior.
- Adoption, access, consumption, activity, prompts, generations, and opened
  pull requests are context. None proves value alone.
- Any improvement is a hypothesis to test. Do not claim universal productivity
  improvement or credit a change to AI without the declared comparison and
  credit limit.
- Unknown evidence remains unknown. Synthetic, placeholder, or illustrative
  fields must not be presented as measured results.
- No CSX or workplace goal is available in the approved public repository
  evidence for this product. Do not include or infer one.
- Existing feedback may shape wording or emphasis but is editorial direction,
  not factual research.
- Do not insert confidential claims, customer data, unpublished workplace
  information, or unsupported consensus.
- The full cost view must name the people and work in scope, time period, source,
  owner, and rule for dividing shared cost. Count each cost once.
