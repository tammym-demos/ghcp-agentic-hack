# Mission Control: Executive AI Value Decision Briefing

This executive product focuses on one pending investment decision. The running
example asks whether to fund a bounded pilot that permits an approved AI coding
assistant to support defined drafting tasks in one team's pull-request-based
production change-control workflow.

The product opens with the decision, tests the evidence and control system, and
ends with an owned ask. **Govern -> Improve -> Prove -> Decide** is wayfinding
through the evidence, not a substitute for that decision-first order.

## Participant outcomes

By 00:45, the executive group can:

1. state the one pending decision and distinguish current activity evidence
   from evidence of an accepted outcome;
2. bound the workflow, define what changes in the pilot and what counts as
   done, and design a fair comparison;
3. require separate technical results and business outcomes, the full pilot
   cost, and named owners, controls, and exceptions;
4. identify contrary evidence, dissent, risks, and the conditions that would
   change the recommendation; and
5. record a stop, revise, fund, or scale decision with a sponsor, resources,
   timeframe, authority, and next gate.

The 12-slide briefing contains **30 instruction minutes**. The protected
executive decision discussion contains **15 discussion minutes**. Mission,
media playback, separate setup/transition, breaks, and contingency are all
**zero**. Total elapsed time is **30 + 15 = 45 minutes**.

No mission, demo, live product environment, account, repository access, or
network connection is required. The deployed 45-slide Mission Control workshop
is unchanged and remains the facilitated governance companion.
