## Estimate arithmetic

- Route: 2 minutes.
- Goal: 5 slides x 6 = 30 minutes.
- Plan: 17 slides x 7 = 119 minutes.
- Build/Evaluate/Deploy/Monitor: 4 x 5 = 20 minutes.
- Return to Goal: 5 minutes.
- Read-back: 4 minutes.
- Cover: 0 minutes.
- Agenda: 0 minutes; an untimed reference, not additional timed instruction.
- Total: 57 framing + 123 discussion = 180 estimated minutes.
- Estimates are not stopwatches; extend, pause, or split delivery to preserve
  evidence quality and discussion.

Every slide has exactly one ordered Timebox, Talk track, Transition, Audience
question, Response guidance, Payoff, Sources notes block. Timebox values match
this estimate for validation; facilitators do not treat them as stopwatches.
