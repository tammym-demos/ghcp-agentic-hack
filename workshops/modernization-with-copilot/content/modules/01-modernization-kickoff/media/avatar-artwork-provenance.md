# Character portrait provenance

The owner selected the circular portrait treatment for this local workshop
draft and accepted the compact Riley framing with her native "Software agent"
label. The owner also selected the seated, camera-facing Purrmission reference
without people or legs in the crop. This visual selection is not local or test
lifecycle approval.

The 512-by-512 PNGs are reusable square rasters; Slidev applies the circular
mask and the name, role, and action as native elements. Morgan, Mergewell, and
Riley are deterministic crops of the approved workshop-local team cover.
Purrmission is a deterministic crop and resample of the approved seated
welcome artwork; no identity, pose, or expression was generated or altered.
The discarded initial cat crop is not used.

The exact source hashes, crop rectangles, destination, background, and output
hashes are recorded in [avatar-artwork-transform.json](./avatar-artwork-transform.json).
The transform manifest is UTF-8 with LF line endings, enforced by Git
attributes. Sidecar manifest hashes refer to those exact portable bytes;
image hashes continue to refer to the unchanged PNG bytes.
Each portrait has an adjacent portable image sidecar. No image-provider call
was made for these derivatives.

The [local source image](./references/purrmission-seated-source.png) for
Purrmission is an unchanged copy of
`workshops/purrmission-to-proceed/content/modules/01-purrmission-to-proceed/public/images/foundation-welcome-trio.png`.
Its original generation record, including the prompt and input reference, is
preserved in [avatar-artwork-purrmission-original.json](./avatar-artwork-purrmission-original.json).
The local source sidecar records the relocated image and original provider
metadata. The team-cover source retains its existing adjacent sidecar.

The characters are teaching cues: the actual sponsor, domain, engineering,
security, operations, data, and release authorities remain people. Riley
returns bounded work for human review; Purrmission reminds people of a
boundary and does not enforce it.
