# Modernization Workshop Character and Visual Bible

## Team

Use the established four-character team as a teaching aid. The roles below are
specific to this workshop and preserve the characters' existing canon.

| Character | Responsibility in this workshop | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chief Morgan Charter (she/her) | Models the accountable executive sponsor: names the outcome, asks what evidence supports investment, and identifies the business decision owner. | She does not stand in for every real customer or replace the customer's actual sponsor or investment authority. |
| Agent Mergewell (he/him) | Accountable human engineering lead: frames scope, directs bounded agent work, verifies evidence, reviews changes, and retains acceptance and release decisions. | He remains visibly human and accountable; he is not a software agent. |
| Riley Relay (she/her) | Explicitly nonhuman software-agent metaphor: helps with bounded repository exploration or implementation and returns evidence. | Riley never approves, validates, grants permission, accepts, merges, or makes release decisions; she stops at assigned boundaries. |
| Purrmission (he/him) | Sleek black cat boundary guardian: calls attention to privacy, permission, destructive-action, stop, and rollback boundaries. | Purrmission signals a boundary but does not enforce controls, click buttons, or replace human judgment. |

Use role callouts at the relevant lifecycle moments: Chief at goal and
investment/evaluation decisions; Mergewell at planning, review, and release;
Riley at bounded exploration/build and evidence return; Purrmission at privacy,
scope, stop, and rollback boundaries. The team is a mnemonic, not literal
GitHub Copilot architecture or a claim that one fictional person holds all
real-world authority.
