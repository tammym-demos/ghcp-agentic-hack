## Claim and publication boundaries

- Persona labels are illustrative context. They do not restrict goal selection
  or imply role authority.
- Named sources and harnesses are neutral labels. Availability, access,
  features, integrations, controls, entitlements, quality, price, and outcomes
  are not asserted.
- Source evidence may include permitted repository/PR activity;
  build/deployment/work-item records; email/Teams/meeting/calendar records; or
  mixed approved sources. It remains in existing source systems.
- The chosen record contains only human review, approval/rejection, material
  exceptions, send/execute authorization, and final action status.
- Agentic workflow patterns may gather, inspect, summarize, draft, prepare,
  detect, propose, or route. They do not autonomously send, merge, deploy,
  remediate, decide test status, or execute another consequential action.
- Attendance does not prove skill or authorize funding, access, AI use,
  communication, implementation, deployment, or action.
- Choice values are fixed publication-safe labels and are still escaped.
  Browser-local state is not identity and is never published.

## Volatile-claim review

No current-product capability claim is required. Generic verbs describe the
participant's bounded workflow, not named-harness behavior. Any statement that
a named harness can access a source, gather, summarize, investigate, draft,
store, review, send, merge, deploy, remediate, decide test status, or integrate
with another system is **unsupported here** and requires Changelog/research
review with durable provenance before publication.
